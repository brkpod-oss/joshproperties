# Josh Properties — Editing guide for the team

This guide explains how to keep the website up to date. Use the login at `https://joshproperties.vercel.app/studio` (you were invited by email). There is **no coding involved** — everything is forms and buttons.

## Quick start (60 seconds)

1. Open Studio and land on the **Dashboard**.
2. Pick a card: *Site settings* for phone/WhatsApp changes, *Home page* for the landing copy, *Properties* to add a listing.
3. Make your edit in the form, tick the fields you may have skipped, then press **Publish**.
4. The website refreshes itself within about a minute. That's it.

## The dashboard

The first screen in Studio links to the seven most-used editors. Everything else is organised in the left menu under these groups:

- **Site settings** (singleton) — brand name, tagline, phone, WhatsApp link, email, address, hours, header menu, footer text.
- **Home page** (singleton) — every word and every section heading on the landing page, plus the **Section visibility** switches to add or remove whole sections.
- **Property page** (singleton) — the small labels used on every listing page (photo gallery, story, key facts, “also in this ground”).
- **Contact page** (singleton) — concierge, office, and form copy.
- **Category pages** — one document per section: Villas, Apartments, Farmlands. Page titles, introduction, masterplan copy.
- **Properties** — all your listings, grouped by category/status/location.
- **Enquiry forms** — the interest and budget chip options on the contact and farmland forms.
- **Farmland projects + Farmland plots** — the interactive masterplan.
- The other types (testimonials, FAQs, stats, etc.) are listed underneath for the related sections.

## Adding a property

1. Open **Properties** and press **Create new**.
2. Fill the *General* tab: name, slug (leave as generated), folio number, category, location, price, area, beds, status.
3. **Publish** tab: turn **Show on homepage** on for carefully chosen listings only, and keep **Published** on (this is what makes it appear on the site).
4. **Photos & video** tab: drag in the main photo and gallery photos, paste a YouTube link for a walkthrough, write the short and full descriptions, and set the **Key facts** points (the table on the page).
5. **SEO** tab: optional search-result title/description and a “hide from Google” switch.
6. Press **Publish**. Done.

To take a property down quietly, just switch **Published** off and publish again — nothing is deleted.

## Editing the forms

The chips on the contact and farmland forms come from **Enquiry forms**:

- A row with group *Interest / Holding* becomes one hub on the **Looking for** selector.
- A row with group *Budget / Timeline* becomes one option under **Budget range**.
- Toggle **Show on the website** off to hide a choice without deleting it. Reorder with the Order number.

The farmland holdings list is the project masterplan (below), so it updates automatically when you edit plots.

## Farmland projects & plots

- **Farmland project** = one masterplan (e.g. “The River Plate”): name, location, total holding, whether a river runs through the plan, and the note under its name.
- **Farmland plot** = one card on the plan: pick its project, give it a short map code (e.g. “A1”), size, phase (1 or 2), status, and position it with X (left-right) and Y (top-bottom) on a 0–100 grid.
- Toggle **Show on the website** off a project to hide that masterplan. The first shown project on the website is the one with the lowest **Order**.

## SEO & sharing

- Site title, search description, keywords, social-sharing image, and the business details used in search engines are all under **Site settings → SEO & Search** and **Legal & RERA**.
- Every page gets its search title/description from its own content (category pages and properties pick it up automatically). You can override a property’s with its **SEO** tab, including hiding it from Google.

## Rules of thumb

- Always press **Publish**. Drafts and un-published documents never reach the website.
- Everything is live-ish: expect a minute before changes appear. If something looks stale, wait and hard-refresh the page.
- If you ever want to revert, use Studio's red **history** button on the document (top-right) to roll back to an earlier version.
